Privacy Policy

LekhaScan
Last updated: 2 September 2026

1. Who We Are

LekhaScan is an Android application published by Dabobroto Sarkar ("we", "us", "our"). For the purposes of applicable data protection law, we are the data controller for the limited processing described in this policy.

Contact: contact@dabobrotosarkar.com

2. Information We Collect

Documents and images. LekhaScan performs optical character recognition entirely on your device. The photographs you capture, the text recognised from them, and the PDF and Word files produced are processed locally and are never transmitted to us. We operate no server capable of receiving them, and we do not collect, store, or have access to your documents at any time.

Generated files are saved to the Downloads/LekhaScan folder on your device and remain under your sole control.

Account information. LekhaScan requires no account, login, or registration. We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number.

3. Information Collected by Third Parties

LekhaScan is provided free of charge and is supported by advertising delivered through Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK is integrated into the app and collects the following automatically:

  • Device and account identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address, from which approximate location may be derived
  • User interactions with advertisements, such as impressions and clicks
  • Device information, including device model and operating system version
  • Diagnostic information, including crash logs and performance data

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us, and is used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. We do not receive it, and we do not sell or share personal information with any other third party.

Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Advertising Privacy Policy. You may reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time through Settings → Privacy → Ads on your device, or opt out of advertising personalisation entirely from the same screen.

4. Network Connections

LekhaScan connects to the internet for two purposes only:

  • Language data. On first use of Bangla or English recognition, the app downloads text recognition model files from the publicly hosted Tesseract OCR project. This is a one-time file download. No information about you or your device is sent as part of this request.
  • Advertising. To request and display advertisements, as described in section 3.

5. Permissions We Request

  • Camera. To photograph the documents you choose to scan.
  • Internet and network state. For the two purposes in section 4.
  • Storage and media access. To save the PDF and Word files you create to your device's Downloads folder.

Each permission is requested at the point it is first needed, and the app explains why before asking.

6. Data Retention

We do not collect or store personal data, so we retain none and there is nothing for us to delete. Data collected by Google for advertising purposes is retained in accordance with Google's own retention policies.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to processing or withdraw consent.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is no account to close and no data for us to export or erase. To exercise rights over advertising data, use the Google controls described in section 3. If you contact us at the address above, we will assist you in directing your request to the appropriate party.

8. Children's Privacy

LekhaScan is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and publish the amended policy at this address before the changes take effect. Material changes will be made clear within the app.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or about how LekhaScan handles information, contact contact@dabobrotosarkar.com.
